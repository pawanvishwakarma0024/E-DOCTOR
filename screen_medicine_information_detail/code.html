Safety Warnings & Triage

emergency Absolute Contraindication

Penicillin / Beta-Lactam Hypersensitivity

Immediate risk of fatal anaphylaxis, Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS), or severe angioedema in patients with documented history of penicillin allergy or cephalosporin cross-reactivity.

science Hepatic Impairment Caution

History of clavulanate-associated cholestatic jaundice requires alternative prescribing. Monitor liver enzymes during extended regimens beyond 14 days.

monitor_heart Renal Clearance Adjustment GFR < 30 mL/min

Do not administer 625 Duo formula to patients with GFR below 30 mL/min due to inappropriate clavulanate to amoxicillin ratio for reduced clearance schedules.

Administration & Storage

thermostat

Store Below 25°C (77°F)

Keep sealed in original protective aluminum blister. Clavulanic acid is hygroscopic and degrades quickly upon ambient moisture exposure.
